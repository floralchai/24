Legally Blonde ♫

Legally Blonde (2001), is one of my favorite movies. Featuring Reese Witherspoon, Selma Blair, Luke Wilson. This movie is about a blonde girl becoming a lawyer. The reason is she just broke up with her boyfriend, Warner. She thought she might be too blonde for him and decided to become a serious law student to prove to him that she is more than that. Elle comes through many glances and hardships to show herself she is worth it.

The logo for the movie "Legally Blonde"


I love the dynamic of Elle with other characters, especially with Paulette and Emmet, a bit of Vivian too if you consider that. I am most concerned about Warner, I don’t like him because he is indecisive and he might even have family pressure to marry Vivian or any other girl that is not like Elle. As for professor Callahan, he might be even worse than Warner. A wolf in sheep clothing if you asked, he has the deception that Vivian is the housewife, as described in the movie, and Elle is just a dumb girl. Gladly at the end of the movie Vivian and Elle became friends and understood each other more. Professor Stromwell is also a great example of first impression is not everything. She might despise Elle at first but at Elle’s worst, such as being harassed. She is there to empowered Elle again.

Also I will pretend that Legally blonde 2 didn’t exist… It breaks all the character development of Elle, just feeling frustrated after seeing the trailer of the sequel itself. Legally blonde will never and ever need a sequel like this. I might sound aggressive about it but I feel like most sequels are meant to fail. I love this movie dearly, my friend recommended it to me and I was satisfied enough.
What? So some people want to say that this movie applied America law into it. Definitely yes. The final scene where Chutney is being accused accurately save the day of Brooke Windham. The first few scenes of Brooke makes her plainly like a failure. But in the end she won her justice, thanks to Elle. Chutney is desperate and sad by how she killed her father and not her stepmother (Brooke Windham) who is somehow the same as her age.
Finally, the rating for Legally Blonde by me would be 8/10. If you enjoy a laid back rom-com with predictable plot points but strive for the character’s success then I would recommend this movie. Not too simple but not too complex is what I would describe.
