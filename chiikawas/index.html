Welcome to Chiikawas!

Chiikawas is a webclique made for Chiikawa fans. We gather people with similiar interests and love for these adorable characters. Currently, the clique is back near the end of 2026. Free to join, and no member list needed.

How to join?

  1. Be nice and kind, it's important to follow
  2. After that save a character to your host. Then, put the code in your website

  3. Welcome you just joined the small clique! Please host the images to your own website
  4. Please do not hotlink the images, host the images to your website
